# v3.4 Adaptive Risk-Control Architecture

## Closed-loop sequence

1. A task arrives with routine, important, or critical class and a class-specific miss target.
2. The runtime observes stale radio, edge, and deadline-tail risk estimates.
3. Only feedback older than the configured delay updates risk debt.
4. Candidate duplication, replica, and migration benefits are ranked under the shared v3.3 credit bucket.
5. The selected action is evaluated against hidden, drifting synthetic probabilities.
6. Its miss is queued and becomes available only after the feedback delay.

The decision path never reads the current task's Bernoulli outcome.

## Global and localized debt

`adaptive_global` applies one scalar debt to every class. Frequent routine tasks therefore dominate its update stream. `adaptive_local` keeps three debts and uses stricter spending gates for routine work and a lower gate for the rare critical class. This is a transparent heuristic reservation mechanism, not a learned policy.

## Metrics beyond the mean

- class-specific and task-weighted miss;
- early/full post-drift miss;
- calibration gap and Brier score;
- maximum rolling weighted miss;
- P95 and CVaR95 latency;
- credits, radio transmissions, replicas, migrations, and action switching;
- active risk debt.

## Non-guarantee

The loop borrows the error-feedback idea of adaptive risk control. Standard conformal guarantees are not imported: losses depend on prior actions, samples are time-dependent, the task mix changes, and the simulator evaluates finite synthetic paths.
